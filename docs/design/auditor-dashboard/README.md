# Auditor dashboard concepts

Three English mobile mockups for Kafaa's Auditor work dashboard. Each uses Seeb Renal Dialysis Centre and fictional audit progress for H2 2026. The three example sections contain four points each.

- [01 Audit card](01-audit-card.png) gives the current audit and its resume action the most space, with a compact section list below.
- [02 Section board](02-section-board.png) gives each selectable section its own card and progress bar.
- [03 Guided checklist](03-guided-checklist.png) shows where to resume and places Previous and Next point on the scoring screen.

Each board shows the start and resume states, all three scoring choices, comments at point, section and audit level, and submission to the Directorate. The submit panel uses a completed lead sign-off as its example state.

Created with the built-in image generation tool.

## Generation prompts

Each image used the shared prompt plus its option-specific direction.

```text
Create one high-fidelity landscape 3:2 product design picture for the mobile Auditor dashboard of Kafaa, a renal dialysis centre quality app. Use the supplied screenshot as a reference only for the app role, the selected centre name and the bottom tabs. Create a new design. Show three large, flat, front-facing columns on a pale blue-gray background: a prominent complete phone-sized Work dashboard on the left, a phone-sized audit scoring screen in the middle, and two stacked state examples on the right. No device hardware, no browser chrome, no purple testing banner, no real user identity, no patient data, no decorative illustrations. The result looks like a professional mobile interface with crisp typography, clinical navy header bars, white card surfaces, cool-blue outlines and muted teal actions, using generous negative space. Use clear title hierarchy and readable text on a phone-sized screen. The numbered concept title is an external board caption, never a line within the app header.
Use the same sample content across the three concepts. LEFT dashboard: app header “Auditor work”. The selected centre is “Seeb Renal Dialysis Centre” for “H2 2026”. An active audit shows “6 of 12 points scored” and a “Resume audit” action. Include an accessible way to select any audit section, with these sample sections and progress, written exactly: “Clinical practice” “2/4”, “Infection prevention” “3/4”, “Safety & equipment” “1/4”. The “Infection prevention” row/card is visually selected or ready to open. At the bottom of the left screen use exactly four existing tabs “Work”, “Audits”, “Actions”, “More”, with Work selected. MIDDLE scoring screen: header “Infection prevention”. Show “Point 2 of 4” and the short audit point “Hand hygiene supplies”. Below, show three large tappable scoring choices, exactly “Met”, “Partially Met” and “Not Met”, with only “Partially Met” selected using a checkmark as well as colour. Do not show numeric scoring weights or invented audit grades. Show a clearly labeled “Point comment” entry tied to the point and a distinct “Section comment” entry tied to Infection prevention. These are audit notes at different levels. RIGHT upper inset: board caption “Before starting”, then a compact early state of the same Work card labeled “Seeb Renal Dialysis Centre” and “H2 2026” with a clearly primary plus or play-button action “Start audit”. RIGHT lower inset: board caption “After completing the audit” then a compact review panel with header “Review & submit”, “12 of 12 points scored”, “Lead sign-off complete”, a field labeled “Overall audit comment” (empty or placeholder “Add a comment”), and a prominent action “Submit to Directorate”. Render as three coherent stages of the same product, with the same typography and corner treatment across panels. Keep all texts spelled exactly with no repetitions, crop, meaningless badges, placeholder lorem ipsum or arrows between screens. The three comment labels and three score choices must all be plainly visible.
```

### 01-audit-card

```text
External board title “01 / Audit card”. On the Work dashboard use a generous deep-navy audit card with Seeb, H2 2026, progress bar, 6 of 12 points scored and a bright Resume audit button within the card. Place a simple three-row “Sections” directory directly below it, with clear selectable rows, progress counts on the right, and a soft teal highlight for Infection prevention. On the scoring screen use a prominent white point card with three stacked, comfortable radio-tile choices. Below the choices, put a multiline Point comment field inside the point card and a separate outlined Section comment row below the card. Right insets repeat the strong primary action style. The distinguishing feature is using a single active-audit card as the front door to the work.
```

### 02-section-board

```text
External board title “02 / Section board”. Work dashboard starts with a slim white selected-centre and period strip, then a compact overall count and Resume audit button. Its most prominent content is a collection of three distinct white clickable section cards, each with its own small completion bar, meaningful outline icon and bold fraction. Use tightly aligned full-width tiles with a blue border and light blue selected treatment on Infection prevention, generous tap targets, and no additional sections. On the middle screen show an open point card for Point 2 of 4 at the centre. Use compact light-background score tiles aligned in a row if text fits, or a clear vertical stack. Show the Section comment action above the point card near the section title. Put a Point comment field inside the point card beneath the scoring choices. Keep right inset cards softly outlined with restrained background tints. The section directory is the visual centre of the option.
```

### 03-guided-checklist

```text
External board title “03 / Guided checklist”. Design the Work dashboard as a step-through flow with a narrow header and a clean grouped progress block showing the selected centre, H2 2026, the 6 of 12 count, and a big Resume audit button next to a line “Continue with point 2”. Under that, place the three selectable sections on a restrained vertical checklist with numbered positions or outline dots, show each progress fraction and highlight Infection prevention as the current section. On the middle phone use a clearly bounded one-point-at-a-time interface. Use a rich navy title, concise Point 2 of 4 progress indicator, a white question card, and 3 comfortably sized score choice rows. Make Point comment and Section comment distinct compact actions attached to their respective scopes. Under the point card put clear “Previous” and “Next point” buttons with simple direction arrows and controlled spacing. In the right column visually echo the action-first flow with a compact Start audit state above a Review & submit state. One calm teal accent and restrained blue-gray framing. The visual signature is guided movement from point to point while retaining direct section access.
```
